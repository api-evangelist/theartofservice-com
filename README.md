The Art of Service is a compliance education and framework intelligence company based in Brisbane, Australia. Founded by Ivanka Menken and Gerard Blokdyk, it offers professional courses, implementation playbooks, toolkits, and a compliance platform that maps controls across regulatory frameworks, enabling organisations to reuse existing evidence and achieve certifications such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, GDPR, and PCI DSS. The platform provides a knowledge base, readiness checks, and cross‑walks between standards, serving enterprises across healthcare, finance, government, and technology sectors.

**Areas:** Compliance · Education · Frameworks · Platform · Australia

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for The Art of Service: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
